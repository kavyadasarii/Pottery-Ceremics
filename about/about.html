Our Story

Where Clay Meets Creativity

ClayCraft was born in a small garage in Banjara Hills in 2014, when founder Meera Nair — a ceramics graduate from NID Ahmedabad — wanted to share her love of clay with Hyderabad. What started as weekend workshops for a handful of friends grew into the city's most beloved pottery studio.

Today, our 3,000 sq ft purpose-built studio hosts over 50 unique classes each week, guided by 12 expert instructors who believe that working with your hands is one of the most grounding, joyful experiences a person can have.

Every bowl, vase, and vessel made here carries the fingerprints of patience, curiosity, and craft. We're not just teaching pottery — we're building a community of makers.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Everything we do at ClayCraft flows from a set of beliefs about creativity, community, and craft.

🤲

Hands-On Always

We believe the best way to learn is by doing. Every class is designed around active making — not watching, not theorising. Get your hands in the clay from minute one.

🌱

Beginner Friendly

No experience needed, ever. Our instructors are trained to meet you exactly where you are and help you create something beautiful regardless of your starting point.

🔥

Quality Craft

We use professional stoneware clays, quality glazes, and fire every piece in our on-site kilns at the correct temperatures — no shortcuts, ever.

🌍

Sustainability

From reclaiming clay scraps to water-recycling systems in our studio, we take our environmental responsibility seriously. Making things slowly is inherently sustainable.

🧘

Mindful Making

Pottery demands presence. In an era of constant distraction, our studio is a space to slow down, breathe, and focus on the thing in front of you.

🤝

Community First

ClayCraft is more than a studio — it's a community of curious, creative people. We host open studios, potluck firings, and monthly community nights.
