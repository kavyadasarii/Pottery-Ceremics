Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need any experience to join? +
Not at all! Most of our classes are designed for complete beginners. We'll walk you through everything from scratch. Just bring your enthusiasm.
What should I wear? +
Wear clothes you don't mind getting dirty — clay has a way of finding everything! We provide aprons but clay splashes happen.
How long until I can take my piece home? +
After your session, your piece needs to dry, be bisque fired, glazed, and glaze fired. This typically takes 10–14 days. We'll notify you when it's ready for collection.
Can I reschedule or cancel a booking? +
Yes! You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before your session for a full credit. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable but can be rescheduled once.
Do you offer gift vouchers? +
Absolutely — gift vouchers are one of our most popular offerings! They're available for any amount or specific class, and never expire. Purchase online or in-studio.
Is parking available at the studio? +
Yes, we have free parking for up to 30 cars in our dedicated lot. We're also a 5-minute walk from the nearest metro station.
