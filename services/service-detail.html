About This Service

Whether you've never touched clay before or want to revisit the basics, our Beginner Pottery class is the perfect starting point. Over 2 guided hours, you'll learn the fundamentals of hand-building techniques and leave with a piece you've made entirely from scratch.

Our instructors create a relaxed, supportive atmosphere — mistakes are part of the process, and every "imperfect" piece has its own charm. Many of our most advanced students started right here.

What You'll Learn

A structured but flexible curriculum designed to build genuine skills, not just produce a souvenir.

01

Introduction to Clay Types

Learn the difference between earthenware, stoneware, and porcelain — and which is best for different projects.

02

Wedging & Preparation

Proper wedging techniques to remove air bubbles and create even consistency throughout your clay.

03

Pinch Pot Fundamentals

The most ancient hand-building technique — create a small vessel using only your hands and patience.

04

Coil Building

Build taller forms by stacking coils of clay. Great for bowls, vases, and sculptural work.

05

Surface Decoration

Stamps, carving, and slip trailing to add texture and personal character to your piece.

06

Glazing Overview

A walkthrough of our glaze palette and how different colours behave in the kiln — so you can choose wisely!
