Quick Answers

Common Questions

Do I need to book in advance? ▾
Yes, we recommend booking at least 2 days ahead as classes fill up quickly, especially on weekends.
What should I wear? ▾
Wear clothes you don't mind getting clay on — it will happen! We provide aprons but clay has a way of going everywhere.
Do you offer gift vouchers? ▾
Yes! Gift vouchers are available for any class or in fixed amounts. Email us or call to arrange one.
Can children attend? ▾
Absolutely — we have dedicated Kids Workshop sessions for ages 6 and up. Adults are also welcome to join alongside their children.
